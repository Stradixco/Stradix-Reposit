Environmental services · Tanker fleet

Liquid and hazardous waste, moved safely by tanker.

Our tanker fleet removes and transports sewage, mixed water, used oil, industrial liquid waste and hazardous liquids — using the latest equipment and secure trucks, with every operation aligned to environmental regulations and the highest standards.

What we transport

The right tanker for every liquid.

From routine wastewater removal to hazardous industrial liquids, each load is matched to a suitable tanker and handled safely, compliantly and on schedule.

  • 01

    Sewage & mixed water

    Safe, compliant removal and transport of sewage and mixed water for reliable, eco-friendly disposal.

  • 02

    Industrial liquid waste

    Secure handling, temporary storage and disposal of industrial liquid waste.

  • 03

    Hazardous liquids & used oil

    Transport of hazardous liquids and eco-friendly disposal of used oils with secure trucks.

  • 04

    Sewer cleaning & suction

    Sewer and drainage cleaning with dedicated vacuum trucks.

  • 05

  • 06

    Water supply by tanker, including fresh drinking water for sites and operations.

How it works

Every trip tracked, every load proven.

Our tanker operations run on ZORA 360, our own operations platform — so each request is dispatched, tracked and closed with evidence.

  1. 01

    Request

    You tell us the site, liquid type and volume.

  2. 02

    Dispatch

    A suitable tanker and crew are assigned.

  3. 03

    Collection

    The load is collected and the quantity recorded on site.

  4. 04

    Transport

    GPS-tracked transport in secure trucks.

  5. 05

    Treatment

    Delivered for treatment or disposal, with GPS evidence of the visit.

  6. 06

    Proof

    Photos and QR-verified service notes close the job.
