About S-TRADIX

Built on expertise. Driven by reliability.

S-TRADIX specialises in environmental services, maintenance, general contracting and the supply of quality materials and spare parts. We also offer specialised technicians, infrastructure solutions, digital products and business establishment support — meeting our clients’ needs efficiently.

S-TRADIX Environmental Services
What we stand for

Environmental at our core, one standard of excellence.

Environmental services

Environmental services are the foundation of S-TRADIX: waste management, waste water and sewage, liquid waste, sewer cleaning, used-oil and mud disposal. Our removal and disposal services use the latest equipment and secure trucks, and we adhere to all environmental regulations — delivering reliable, eco-friendly solutions to the highest standards.

General contracting & infrastructure

S-TRADIX is a trusted partner in general contracting, providing comprehensive solutions in the design and execution of major infrastructure projects. We manage every aspect — planning, design and execution — while adhering to timelines and budgets, and we provide business establishment support so clients can launch with confidence and stability.
