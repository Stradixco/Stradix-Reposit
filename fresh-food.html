From Egyptian farms to every city in the Kingdom.

S-TRADIX Food Supply imports fresh vegetables, fruits, herbs and spices from Egypt and supplies our clients across all cities of Saudi Arabia — harvested, sorted, packed and delivered through an integrated cold chain, in line with Saudi specifications and each client’s requirements.

Fresh, all year round

Fresh produce, sourced at the farm and delivered to your door.

We source from farms in Egypt to bring every type of fresh vegetable and fruit — together with herbs and spices — to clients in Saudi Arabia. Produce is selected and packed to Saudi market specifications and to each client’s order, then moved through refrigerated transport for timely, reliable delivery.
